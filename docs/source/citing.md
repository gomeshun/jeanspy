# Citing JeansPy and its dependencies

Until a paper and archival release DOI are available, cite the repository and
the exact commit used. Do not cite an unpublished manuscript as an accepted
paper. A suitable development-software reference is:

> Horigome, S., JeansPy, development source at commit
> 1a0ad4028d26af1df389ebdfdf992285ec50f8bb,
> https://github.com/gomeshun/jeanspy.

Also cite the scientific methods, data and software actually used. The
axisymmetric methods are described by
[Hayashi & Chiba (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07620) and
[Hayashi et al. (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08046).
Finite-cone spherical J factors follow
[Ullio & Valli (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.07721).
The Draco spectroscopy is from
[Walker et al. (2015)](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/448/3/2717/1091040).
For comparisons, retain the cited paper and exact code version together.

The [reference list](references.md) and its downloadable BibTeX file include
the comparison literature, profile definitions, data and sampling methods.
The extended Sersic deprojection coefficients come from Appendix A of
[Vitral & Mamon (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202039650), building
on [Vitral & Mamon (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201937202).

catalogues have their own licenses and attribution requirements.
